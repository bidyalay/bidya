# 📗 English II

## Overview
English language course - Book 2

## Contents
- Advanced grammar
- Literature
- Communication skills
- Creative writing

## Learning Resources

### Grammar
- Learn verb tenses: present, past, and future.
- Learn subject and predicate.
- Use articles **a, an, the** correctly.
- Learn conjunctions such as **and, but, because, or**.
- Use punctuation to make writing clear.

### Literature
- Read stories, poems, and other texts carefully.
- Notice the characters, setting, problem, and main message.
- Find new words and learn their meanings.

### Communication
- Listen carefully when someone speaks.
- Speak clearly and politely.
- Ask questions when you do not understand.

### Creative Writing
- Start with a clear idea.
- Put events in a good order.
- Add details that help the reader imagine the scene.

### Quick Practice
1. Write one sentence in past tense.
2. Make a sentence using “because.”
3. Name two parts of a story.
4. Write a short paragraph about your hobby.
