## Learning Resources

### Number and Arithmetic
- Know place value and compare large numbers.
- Practice addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.
- Learn factors, multiples, HCF, and LCM.
- Understand fractions, decimals, ratios, and percentages.

### Algebra
- A letter can represent an unknown number.
- Learn simple expressions such as **3x + 2**.
- Solve simple equations by finding the unknown value.

### Geometry
- A line segment has two endpoints.
- An angle is formed when two lines or rays meet.
- Learn triangles, quadrilaterals, circles, perimeter, and area.

### Measurement
- Common units include metre, centimetre, kilogram, and litre.
- Perimeter is the distance around a shape.
- Area measures the surface inside a shape.

### Quick Practice
1. Find the HCF of 12 and 18.
2. Write 3/4 as a decimal.
3. If x + 5 = 12, find x.
4. Find the perimeter of a square with side 6 cm.
