## Learning Resources

### Human Body
- The body has many organs that work together.
- The heart pumps blood around the body.
- The lungs help us breathe.
- The brain helps control the body and helps us think.

### Nutrition
- A balanced diet includes different kinds of healthy foods.
- Important nutrients include carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, fibre, and water.
- Drink enough clean water and eat a variety of foods.

### Personal Hygiene
- Wash hands with soap, especially before eating and after using the toilet.
- Brush your teeth regularly.
- Keep your body, clothes, and surroundings clean.

### Physical and Mental Well-being
- Be active through age-appropriate play and exercise.
- Get enough sleep and rest.
- Talk to a trusted adult when you feel worried or need help.
