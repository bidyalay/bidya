# 🌏 Samajik (Social Studies)

## Overview
Social studies and cultural education

## Contents
- Geography
- History
- Culture and society
- Civics and government

## Learning Resources

### Geography
- Geography helps us understand places, land, water, climate, and people.
- Learn to read simple maps using directions and symbols.
- The four main directions are north, south, east, and west.

### History
- History tells us about people and events from the past.
- A timeline puts events in the order they happened.
- Learn about important people and events of Nepal.

### Culture and Society
- Nepal has many languages, cultures, festivals, and traditions.
- Respect people who have different customs and backgrounds.
- Good communities work together and help one another.

### Civics
- Citizens have rights and responsibilities.
- Follow rules, respect others, and help keep the community clean and safe.
- Learn how local and national government serves people.

### Quick Practice
1. Name the four main directions.
2. What is a timeline?
3. Why should we respect different cultures?
4. Write two responsibilities of a good citizen.
