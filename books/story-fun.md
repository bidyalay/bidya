## Learning Resources

### How to Read a Story
Look for:
- **Characters:** Who is in the story?
- **Setting:** Where and when does it happen?
- **Problem:** What challenge happens?
- **Events:** What happens first, next, and last?
- **Message:** What can we learn from the story?

### Fables and Fairy Tales
- Fables often use animals to teach a lesson.
- Fairy tales may include imaginary places or magical events.
- Think about the lesson instead of only remembering the ending.

### Adventure Stories
- Notice the main character's goal and the challenges they face.
- Put important events in the correct order.

### Creative Reading
- Imagine what a character may be thinking or feeling.
- Learn new words from the story.
- Retell the story in your own words.

### Quick Practice
1. Name the main character in a story you read.
2. Write the setting of the story.
3. What lesson did you learn?
4. Retell the story in five sentences.
