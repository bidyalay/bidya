## Learning Resources

### Daily Life Skills
- Keep your study and living space tidy.
- Organize books and school supplies.
- Learn to plan simple daily tasks.
- Use household items carefully and safely.

### Helping at Home
- Help with age-appropriate household tasks.
- Put things back after using them.
- Save water and electricity.
- Keep shared spaces clean.

### Family and Relationships
- Speak politely and listen to family members.
- Share responsibilities and help others.
- Solve small disagreements calmly.

### Hobby Projects
- Try drawing, reading, gardening, simple crafts, or another safe hobby.
- Make a small project and write the steps you followed.

### Quick Practice
1. Write three ways you can help at home.
2. Why should we save water and electricity?
3. Name one hobby project you would like to try.
