# 📖 Nepal Bhasa

## Overview
Nepal Bhasa (Newari) language course

## Contents
- Nepal Bhasa alphabets
- Vocabulary and phrases
- Cultural heritage
- Traditional texts

## Learning Resources

### Language Basics
- Practice the letters and their sounds regularly.
- Learn a few useful words and phrases each day.
- Read words aloud to improve pronunciation.
- Make short sentences with new words.

### Vocabulary Practice
- Keep a small word list in your notebook.
- Write the word, meaning, and an example.
- Review old words every week.

### Culture and Heritage
- Learn about Nepal Bhasa traditions, festivals, art, and history.
- Respect and value different languages and cultures.
- Ask elders or teachers about traditional words and customs.

### Quick Practice
1. Write five Nepal Bhasa words you know.
2. Learn two new phrases and use them in sentences.
3. Write one thing you learned about Nepal Bhasa culture.
