## Learning Resources

### Grammar
- A **noun** names a person, place, animal, or thing.
- A **pronoun** is used in place of a noun.
- A **verb** shows an action or state.
- An **adjective** describes a noun.
- A sentence usually begins with a capital letter and ends with punctuation.

### Vocabulary
- Learn 5 new words each day.
- Write the meaning and make one sentence with each new word.
- Learn synonyms and antonyms.

### Reading
- Read a short passage slowly.
- Find the main idea and important details.
- Answer questions using complete sentences.

### Writing
- Plan your ideas before writing.
- Use clear sentences and correct punctuation.
- Check spelling after writing.

### Quick Practice
1. Find the noun and verb in: “The boy runs.”
2. Write an adjective for “flower.”
3. Write three new words and their meanings.
4. Write five sentences about your school.
