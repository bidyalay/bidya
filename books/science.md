## Learning Resources

### Physics
- **Force** is a push or pull.
- **Motion** means a change in position with time.
- Light helps us see objects.
- Sound is produced by vibrations.

### Chemistry
- **Matter** is anything that has mass and takes up space.
- The three common states of matter are solid, liquid, and gas.
- Water can change between these states by heating or cooling.

### Biology
- Living things grow, breathe, reproduce, and respond to their surroundings.
- Plants make food mainly by photosynthesis.
- A balanced diet gives our body the nutrients it needs.

### Earth Science
- Earth has land, water, air, and living things.
- The water cycle includes evaporation, condensation, and precipitation.
- Weather can change from day to day.
